Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-11

This application is for personal automation. It accesses Google data only after explicit consent and only for the account owner's requested tasks.

Permissions cover reading Gmail; reading and managing authorized Analytics and Search Console resources; verifying site ownership; and submitting eligible indexing requests.

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's private server, with a private local backup. Task outputs may be stored by the owner as required for personal workflows. Credentials and Google user data are not collected by this public website, sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account permissions. The owner can remove locally retained credentials and task data.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
